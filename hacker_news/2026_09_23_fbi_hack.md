# When did this happen?
September 22nd, 2026 (Reuters)
# Which website was affected?
`FBIjobs.gov`, specifically the `apply` subdomain
# Who is the victim?
The FBI
# Who was the attacker?
The ShinyHunters cybercriminal gang
# What was the impact?
* [Webpage defacement](https://www.bleepstatic.com/images/news/security/attacks/f/fbi/shinyhunters-defacement-data-breach-allegedly/fbijobs-defacement.jpg)
* the ShinyHunters claim to have stolen 2-3 TB worth of FBI data which includes:
  * PII / PHI on employees and job applicants (BleepingComputer)
  * information on the employees' spouses (404 Media)
# What was the motivation?
* The ShinyHunters claim that the defacement was in retaliation for a public service announcement that featured the ShinyHunters group by name and said that they were engaging in extortion and harassment (Bleeping Computer)
* The ShinyHunters demand that the FBI retract their statements on their group within 1 week (Bleeping Computer)
# The Modus Operandi
* the group claims to have compromised the FBI website after exploiting an Oracle PeopleSoft vulnerability via zero-day (Bleeping Computer)
  * PeopleSoft is human-resource and financial software
